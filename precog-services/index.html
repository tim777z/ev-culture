FAQ

How does payment work?

Quote and confirm via email (mailto buttons above). Card-gated checkout (Stripe/PayPal) links are placeholders until configured per README.md; the operator supplies card details in-session for any paid signup and never shares them by email.

Do you issue SOC 2 / compliance certifications?

No. PreCog Security provides readiness support and assessments. Formal attestations are issued by external accredited auditors only.

Can I see a sample before paying?

Yes — that is our default. Ask for a redacted sample report for your vertical via email and we will send one before any charge.

Where is my data handled?

Engagement data is handled under NDA with a liability cap in the MSA. We do not sell or share customer data, and we minimise retention of credentials and PHI.
